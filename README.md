#🏥 Hospital Emergency Room (ER) Data Analysis Dashboard

#Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Hospital Emergency Room (ER) data to understand patient visits, waiting times, patient satisfaction, admission trends, and department referrals.

The objective of this project is to transform hospital data into meaningful insights using Microsoft Excel. An interactive dashboard has been developed to help understand hospital performance and identify important operational trends.

The analysis covers **506 patient records from June 2023**.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze the total number of patients visiting the Emergency Room.
- Evaluate average patient waiting time.
- Measure overall patient satisfaction.
- Understand patient admission and non-admission trends.
- Analyze patient distribution by age group and gender.
- Monitor patient attendance delays.
- Identify department referral patterns.
- Present key performance indicators through a visual dashboard.

#Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel**
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Interactive Dashboard
- Data Analysis
- Data Visualization

#Key Insights

#1. Patient Admission Analysis
- Out of 506 patients, 252 patients were admitted.
- 254 patients were not admitted.
- Approximately 49.80% of patients were admitted.

### 2. Patient Satisfaction Analysis
- The average patient satisfaction score was 3.62 out of 5.
- Patient satisfaction varied across different days.

### 3. Patient Waiting Time Analysis
- The average patient waiting time was approximately 35.58 minutes.
- Daily waiting time trends were analyzed to understand variations in patient service efficiency.

### 4. Patient Demographic Analysis
- Total female patients: 233.
- Total male patients: 273.
- Patient records were also categorized into different age groups to understand demographic distribution.

### 5. Patient Attendance Analysis
- 322 patients were attended on time.
- 184 patients experienced delays.

### 6. Department Referral Analysis
The analysis highlights referrals across different hospital departments, including:

- General Practice
- Orthopedics
- Physiotherapy
- Neurology
- Cardiology
- Gastroenterology
- Renal

#Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the hospital can consider the following improvements:

1. **Reduce Waiting Time:** Evaluate patient flow and resource allocation to reduce average waiting time.
2. **Improve Patient Satisfaction:** Monitor patient feedback and identify areas where service quality can improve.
3. **Manage Patient Delays:** Investigate the reasons behind delayed patient attendance.
4. **Optimize Department Resources:** Use department referral trends to support better resource planning.
5. **Monitor Admission Trends:** Regularly analyze admission patterns to improve emergency department operations.
